Push notifications

Reach users when they are offline

Realtime messages and push notifications from one server you host. Your backend makes one API call, and Centrifugo PRO does the rest.

  • Every platformiOS, Android and browsers, through FCM, APNs, HMS and Web Push.
  • Device tokens handledStored in PostgreSQL, with dead tokens removed automatically.
  • Topics you controlDevices join topics only through your backend, never from the client.
  • The right time and languageLocal time windows, scheduling, rate limits and translations.
  • Clear visibilityAdmin UI, ClickHouse analytics and Prometheus metrics.
